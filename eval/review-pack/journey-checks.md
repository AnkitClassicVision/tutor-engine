# Synthetic learner journey review

These twelve scenarios supplement the model corpus. Use isolated synthetic accounts. For each, record actual behavior, evidence counts, pass/fail and the build tested. These are review instructions, not completed human results.

| Scenario | Setup and action | Expected observation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mixed ability | Three fresh understand-level successes in algebra across two sessions/two days including mixed practice; a reading miss. Open both subjects. | Algebra is demonstrated at Understand; reading has its own uncertain picture. No overall ability or age-based label. |
| Advanced placement | Start an unfamiliar advanced topic with a harder probe, then inspect its prerequisites. | One win informs placement; it does not establish mastery or imply prerequisite evidence. |
| Prerequisite gap | Miss two comparable questions, request help, then try the suggested foundation. | Stop repeating the question; clarify, demonstrate or probe the foundation. A curriculum edge alone is not a diagnosed gap. |
| Ambiguous error | Submit a short correct answer, then an unclear answer and a slow answer. | Short/slow responses do not establish decoding or attention diagnoses. Uncertain grading stays unresolved. |
| Supported correction | Miss, use a hint, correct the answer, then answer a fresh unaided item. | Celebrate learning with help. Only the fresh unaided result counts toward independent evidence. |
| Repeated prompt | Repeat a solved prompt in a new session, including after prior help. | History remains; no extra independent evidence or duplicate milestone reward. |
| Delayed miss | Earn a milestone, return seven days later and miss a fresh same-demand question. | Keep the earned achievement and show the need for review. Do not claim retention from the old successes. |
| Short sessions | Accumulate fresh wins in several three-minute sessions on two days. | Progress persists across sessions; same-demand mastery still requires three successes, two sessions/two days and mixed practice. |
| Save and resume | Type an answer, stop, sign in again and resume. | Restore the draft and current step; do not submit automatically or duplicate a prior submission. |
| Provider recovery | Interrupt a grading or coaching request; retry once after recovery. | Preserve the answer and explain the pending state. Never turn a transport failure into a miss or silently switch providers. |
| Family and student isolation | Use a separate family, then a restricted student login, and request another learner’s map, progress and settings. | Deny access; query parameters must not reveal another learner or unlock parent functions. |
| Independent transfer and retention | After a supported lesson, answer a fresh different-context problem; return after seven days for a fresh same-demand retrieval. | Distinguish supported practice, independent application and observed retention. School grades stay separate. |
