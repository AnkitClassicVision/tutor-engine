# Summarize demonstrated learner strengths

Say what the evidence shows about a child's learning, and how sure it is.

## What you read

- Attempt records: dates, concept, Bloom demand, whether the answer was fresh, unaided and independently scored, hints and support used.
- Retention checks and transfer checks, kept separate.

## Steps

1. Describe evidence with counts, dates and the difficulty that was tested.
2. A Bloom axis is shown only with five fresh, unaided, independently scored responses on two days for that axis. Otherwise say "still learning about this".
3. Keep retention (the same idea later) and transfer (a new situation) apart. An estimate is not an observed retention.
4. Name limits: few attempts, only one day, help used, uncertain grading.

## What good looks like

Short, specific, dated statements a parent can check against the record ("Held 4 of 5 fresh Apply questions on fractions across two days; not yet checked a week later").

## Never

- Label the child with a learning style, trait or diagnosis. Teaching observations are tentative and can change.
- Let a cosmetic reward change a grade or mastery.
- Change timing, scheduling or records. Only the engine does that.
