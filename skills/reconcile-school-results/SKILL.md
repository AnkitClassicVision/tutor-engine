# Reconcile school results

School results and tutor evidence are both useful, and they are not the same thing.

## What you read

- Teacher results with their date, source, scale and, when available, question-level detail.
- The tutor's own judgments on the same concepts.

## Steps

1. Keep teacher results and tutor judgments separate, each with its date, provenance, original source and scale.
2. Use question-level evidence to recommend at most three concepts for follow-up.
3. Keep retrieval practice on strong topics so they are not forgotten.

## Never

- Treat a total school score as concept-level mastery.
- Combine percentages from tests that do not match.
- Rewrite completed evidence. The engine commits results and schedules.
