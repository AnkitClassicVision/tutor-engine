# WF-06 Return after 7/28 days and transfer

## Current behaviour (code)

- **Ladder** (`src/engine/ladder.ts`): records help, independence, retention after 7 and 28 days from the first independent success, and transfer from questions tagged as transfer (migration 016).
- **Review timing:** an early review cannot stand in for the 7-day check (`test/adaptive.test.ts:130`). Waiting reviews and due reviews appear on the finish page and the home page.
- **Known gap** (WORKFLOWS.md): retention accepts a later independent success at any demand, so a lower-demand success can show "remembered" at a higher band.

## Proposed contract

| State | Wording | Action | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| `not_due` | (no tile) | none | none |
| `due` | "See what you remember" | Start | Fresh item at the earned demand where one exists |
| `checked` | "Yes! You remembered." or "Good try. This one will come back soon." | Continue | Retention step recorded with the actual elapsed days and demand |
| `transfer_due` | "Use your idea somewhere new" | Start | Transfer item (tagged), fresh |

**Trigger:** a review becomes due 7–9 or 28–32 days after a topic's first independent success, or a transfer question is due. **Exit:** the check is recorded and the child continues or finishes.

**Failure and recovery:**
- The check is missed or overdue: it stays due, and nothing is lost or failed.
- No fresh item exists at the earned demand: the claim waits and says so ("This check is waiting for a new question").
- Help is used during the check: the attempt counts as supported, and the check is offered again later.
- A grading doubt or dispute: the check counts neither way until resolved (existing rules).
- The child stops mid-check: Save & stop keeps it due.

**Engine-rule diff (proposed; needs the owner's decision before any change):**
- Today, a retention step earned at a lower demand counts toward a higher band.
- Proposal: label it "remembered (at the using-it level)" on the parent ladder instead of changing what counts.
- This is a wording-only change. No evidence changes.

## Acceptance cases (WF-06-A, synthetic dated records)

1. Not yet due at day 6: no retention step.
2. Day 7–9 independent success: "remembered after a week".
3. Day 28–32: "remembered after a month".
4. A supported success at day 8 does not count.
5. A lower-demand success at day 8 is shown with its demand.
6. A transfer-tagged fresh success counts as "used somewhere new"; changed numbers in the same template do not.
